\caption{Per-cohort performance across the four folds. Each cell gives the mean, the half-width of the 95\% confidence interval across folds (Student $t$, $df=3$), and the standard deviation in parentheses; leading zeros are omitted. Gene-level PCC and SCC are correlations across slides computed per gene and averaged over genes. Pathway metrics are the median over gene sets of the per-set Spearman correlation, aggregated from gene-level predictions (pathway labels are never used for training). Intervals are computed independently per method, so overlapping intervals do not imply the absence of a difference: every method sees the same folds and the same held-out slides, and method-to-method comparisons should be paired. Best value per row in bold.}
